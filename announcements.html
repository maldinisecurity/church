Pastoral Care

Be Your Brother’s Keeper

If you know of anyone that has not been in church for more than two weeks, please call or visit them and encourage them to return.

Hebrews 10:25: “Not giving up meeting together, as some are in the habit of doing, but encouraging one another.”

If there is a reason they are unable to attend church, please speak to the pastor and we will contact them.

Your care keeps the family strong.
