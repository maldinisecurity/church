Nativity Narration

Danny & Tracy — Christmas Service 2025
Block 1 • Tracy

Prophetic Promise

  • Verses: Isaiah 9:6–7; Micah 5:2; Psalm 98
  • Carols: Glorious; Hosanna in Excelsis; Joy to the World

Long before the manger, the prophets gave clues. Isaiah spoke about a child who would bring peace. Micah pointed to a small town that would host a big moment. Psalms cheered about God’s power and joy. It sounded like God set the stage and dropped hints everywhere. So tonight we cheer, because the promise didn’t stay as words. It came alive.

Block 2 • Danny

Heaven Breaks In

  • Verses: Luke 2:8–14
  • Carols: The First Noel; Angels from the…

Picture some tired shepherds trying to mind their business. Then the sky lit up, and angels broke into song like heaven started a concert without warning. The shepherds jumped, blinked and probably asked, “Is this for us?” Yes, it was. A Saviour arrived, and heaven couldn’t keep quiet. That night turned a normal field into the loudest announcement ever.

Block 3 • Tracy

The Guiding Star

  • Verses: Matthew 2:1–2
  • Carols: Angels We Have Heard on High; Ding Dong Merrily on High

Far away, some wise men checked the sky and spotted a star that didn’t behave like the rest. They packed gifts, grabbed their charts and followed it. No sat-nav, no Google map, no traffic updates, just a bright sign pulling them forward. The star pointed to a newborn King, so they moved with faith and excitement.

Block 4 • Danny

Name Above Names

  • Verses: Matthew 1:21
  • Carols: Silent Night; Hark the Herald

Joseph faced a huge moment. An angel told him the child would save people. That name, Jesus, carried purpose from the start. So Joseph stood firm and protected his family. The baby arrived in the quiet of the night, but the meaning of his name shook the world.

Block 5 • Tracy

The Word Became Flesh

  • Verses: John 1:14
  • Carols: O Holy Night; O Come All Ye Faithful

God didn’t stay far. He came close. He came in a way we could see and touch. Grace took a human form and walked among us. That’s why we lift our voices tonight. The light of God stepped right into our world and brought hope that still stands.

Final Block • Danny & Tracy

Celebrate & Share

  • Celebration Set: Let’s Rejoice; We Wish You a Merry Christmas; Feliz Navidad; Hallelujah Duet; Go Tell It on the Mountain (Calypso)

Danny: We’ve heard the story from promise to birth. Now it’s time to cheer. The Saviour came for everyone, so the joy shouldn’t stay in one place. Share it, sing it and spread it with energy. Christmas carries good news, and you carry the message.

Tracy & Danny: We wish you a merry Christmas and a happy new year in advance.
